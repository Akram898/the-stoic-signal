# SHORT 3 — "Your Anger Is Their Remote Control"
*Pillar: Discipline Tactics / Modern Application · ~32–36s · THE STOIC SIGNAL*

## Thesis
Whoever makes you angry owns your next move. Stoic calm is not passivity — it is refusing to hand someone the controller.
